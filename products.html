FAQ

Common Questions

How do I get the indicators?
All six are free and public on TradingView. Open the BlueBeck profile, add any indicator to your favorites, and drop it on a chart — no purchase, no invite, no waiting.
Do I need a paid TradingView plan?
A free TradingView account is enough to use the tools on a chart. The Pine Screener (used by Target Isolator to scan a watchlist) is a TradingView feature — screener size and options vary by plan, but the basic scan works on free accounts. Where the free plan bites is indicator count: it caps how many studies you can stack on one chart, so running several BlueBeck tools side by side is where a paid plan starts to earn its keep. If you decide to go that route, here's my TradingView link.
Are these really free? What's the catch?
No catch. I'm releasing these free tools to build the BlueBeck brand and earn trust before asking anyone to pay for anything. Every feature is included — nothing is locked.
Do these work for any stock?
Yes. The tools work on any stock or ETF available on TradingView, on any timeframe. They're built for daily swing trading but nothing limits their use.
Will there be more tools?
Yes — Pro versions of Swing Trade Scanner, Watchlist Curator, and Swing Trade Defender are in development: trade levels and the short side on the Scanner, a whole-market bellwether board on the Curator, and an eight-position monitor on the Defender. The free versions stay free. Follow the BlueBeck profile on TradingView to catch the launches.
What broker do you trade through?
Robinhood. No commissions, fractional shares so I can size a position properly instead of rounding to whole shares, and it's easy to check on a phone. The BlueBeck tools work with any broker; they live on the chart, not the order ticket.
