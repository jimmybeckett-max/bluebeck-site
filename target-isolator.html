What it does

One number. Five things checked. Instant answer.

Target Isolator answers one question for any stock: is this a swing setup worth my time? It rolls the five things swing traders check into a single 0–100 Score — so you can scan fast and focus on the leaders.

  • Score line (bottom pane) — a live 0–100 chart you can watch trend over time. Rising score = improving setup, often before price makes it obvious.
  • Dashboard (on the chart) — breaks the score into its parts: group strength, stock RS, swing state, weekly confirm, freshness, liquidity, and a ✓ Pass flag.
  • Pine Screener output — every metric is a column you can sort and filter across your full watchlist.

The Score

Five things swing traders check — in one number.

Each component is weighted. They add up to the Score. A reading above 65 is the strong zone.

1
Group Strength
Is the stock's sector or industry leading the market vs SPY?
2
Relative Strength
Is the stock itself outperforming the S&P 500?
3
4-MA Ribbon
Is price in a clean, stacked uptrend across four moving averages?
4
Weekly Confirm
Does the weekly chart agree with the daily setup?
5
Liquidity
Enough price and dollar-volume to enter and exit cleanly?
The power move

Screen your whole watchlist at once.

Most free indicators can't do this. Target Isolator runs in the TradingView Pine Screener and grades every stock on your watchlist in one scan — no chart-flipping required.

  1. 1 Click the ★ to add Target Isolator to your favorites — the Screener only lists favorited scripts.
  2. 2 Build a watchlist of the names you follow.
  3. 3 Open the Pine Screener, pick your watchlist, and select Target Isolator.
  4. 4 Click Score to sort high → low. Filter Pass = 1 and your strongest setups rise to the top.

The Screener runs on the daily timeframe independently of your chart. Timeframe options and scan size depend on your TradingView plan.

Settings

Defaults work out of the box.

Tuned for daily swing trading from the start. Adjust when you want the tool to match your own style.

4-MA ribbon lengths
Match the moving averages you already trade. Defaults are EMA 10 / 20 / 50 / 100.
Default: 10 / 20 / 50 / 100
RS lookback
How far back relative strength is measured against SPY.
Default: 60 bars
Higher-timeframe confirm
Toggle on/off. Which timeframe confirms the daily setup — weekly by default.
Default: Weekly, on
Liquidity filter
Minimum price and average dollar-volume thresholds to screen out illiquid names.
Default: $10 price / $10M vol
Composite weights
How much each of the five components counts toward the final Score.
Adjustable per component
Table position & size
Move the dashboard anywhere on the chart. Affects the on-chart view only — not the Screener output.
Default: Top Right
