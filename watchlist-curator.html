What it does

From strong sector to five names worth your time.

You've spotted the strong sector. Now — which stocks? Watchlist Curator takes one sector, scores ten of its biggest, most-traded names against the same yardstick, and hands you the top five in ranked order.

  • Ten bellwethers per sector — each of the 11 sectors carries a fixed basket of household names (Technology = AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, AVGO and so on). It ranks within that basket — honest about what it scans.
  • Leadership Score — relative strength vs SPY is the backbone, plus points for the trend stack, the RSI sweet spot, and volume. Hover the Score header on the chart for the full breakdown.
  • RSI sweet spot (50–70) — a red-hot name that's already stretched ranks lower on purpose, so the list points at leaders you can still enter, not chases.
  • Rating column — Strong / Good / Fair / Lagging translates the score at a glance. The #1 row is highlighted in gold.
  • Built to pair with Sector Sweeper — the sector dropdown literally says "set to Sector Sweeper's #1."

How to use it

Three steps to a focused watchlist.

1

Find the leading sector

Sector Sweeper — also free — ranks all 11 sectors for you. Whatever sits at #1 is where money is flowing right now.

2

Pick it in the dropdown

Set the Curator's sector dropdown to that group. It scores the basket and ranks the leaders instantly.

3

Study the top five

The gold row is the current leader. Those five charts are the ones worth your study time — grade each one with Swing Trade Scanner.

Settings

Defaults work out of the box.

Pick your sector and it's ready. Tune when you want to.

Sector & timeframe
The sector dropdown has all 11 choices. The ranking uses its own timeframe no matter what chart you're on.
Default: Daily
Score inputs
RS/momentum lookback, fast/slow EMA for the trend stack, and RSI length — the yardstick every name is measured with.
Default: lookback 20 · EMA 20/50 · RSI 14
Stocks to show
How many ranked names appear in the table, up to five. All ten are always scored — you see the true leaders.
Default: 5
Table & theme
9 table positions, four text sizes, dark/light theme, and colors — set it up for your screen.
Default: Dark theme
