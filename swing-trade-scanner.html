What it does

One honest answer: score, grade, verdict.

You've found a stock. Is it actually a setup? Swing Trade Scanner grades the chart in front of you the way a swing trader reads it — trend, momentum, relative strength, volume — and answers in plain English.

  • 0–100 score — five weighted checks: MA alignment (up to 35 pts), momentum (20), distance from the recent high (20), relative strength vs SPY (15), and volume (10).
  • Grade + Verdict — HIGH QUALITY, MODERATE, WEAK, AVOID, or EXTENDED / WAIT. No decoding required.
  • Extension penalty — more than 6% above the fast MA and the score gets docked. Chasing a stretched chart is how good setups become bad entries.
  • 4-MA ribbon on the chart — the trend zone shades green in an uptrend, red in a downtrend. Optional candle coloring shows the stack state at a glance.
  • Long setups only — said plainly — on a downtrending chart it shows Full Bear and AVOID. It won't invent an opportunity.

How to use it

Grade the chart before you commit a dollar.

1

Pull up your candidate

From Watchlist Curator's top five, a Target Isolator scan, or your own list — open the chart and drop the Scanner on it.

2

Read the grade

Score and grade sit up top. The four metric rows — MA Alignment, Momentum, RS vs SPY, Volume — show exactly what's earning or costing points.

3

Respect the verdict

HIGH QUALITY means everything lined up. EXTENDED / WAIT means good stock, bad moment — let it pull in. AVOID means look elsewhere.

Settings

Defaults work out of the box.

Add it to your chart and it's ready — no configuration needed to start. Tune when you want to.

MA ribbon
Four moving averages define the trend. Choose the lengths, the type (EMA, SMA or WMA), and the colors.
Default: EMA 20 / 50 / 100 / 200
Lookback (bars)
The window used for momentum, relative strength, and the recent high. 60 bars on a daily chart is roughly three months.
Default: 60 bars
Volume MA length
The average that current volume is compared against for the volume check.
Default: 20 bars
Table & theme
9 dashboard positions, four text sizes, dark/light theme, and status colors — set it up for your screen.
Default: Dark theme
